Who We Are

Gen-Z Kalyan Seva is a non-profit organization driven by youth, hoping towards the development of underprivileged communities. We refuse to just watch from the sidelines. Our core focus lies in providing critical health resources, quality education, and nutritious food to poor children who need it the most. We bridge the gap between people, purpose, and a better tomorrow.

Zero Hunger

Providing daily nutritious meals and rations to children living on the streets, ensuring no child goes to sleep hungry.

Education for All

Distributing books, stationery, and offering free foundational classes so that financial status doesn't dictate a child's future.

Health & Hygiene

Conducting health camps, distributing clean clothing, and teaching basic sanitation to build stronger, healthier communities.
